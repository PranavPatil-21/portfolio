## What was deliberately NOT taken

- **The scrubbed hero video and profile photograph.** Those are footage and
  images of Sarang, a real person. Reusing someone's likeness on a different
  person's portfolio would misrepresent both of them, regardless of what the
  code licence permits. The hero here is a particle portrait generated from the
  site owner's own photograph instead.
- **The Supabase / MongoDB / Resend backend.** The reference stores its blog,
  comments and analytics in a hosted database. This site is deliberately
  database-free: content lives as files in the repository, so there is nothing
  to pay for and nothing that expires while the site sits untouched.
- **All written content.** Every word here is the site owner's own.
