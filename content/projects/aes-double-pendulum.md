Designed an AES encryption system with chaos-driven key generation using
double-pendulum dynamics to enhance entropy, randomness, and resistance to key
predictability.

The double pendulum is a classic chaotic system: arbitrarily small differences
in initial conditions diverge exponentially. Sampling its state space gives a
source of entropy that is deterministic to reproduce given the seed, yet
practically unpredictable without it.
